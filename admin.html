Add a Contact

Bulk add multiple contacts at once

Start with a header row naming your columns, in any order: Business Name, First Name, Last Name, Phone, Industry, Email (a single Contact Name column works too instead of First/Last). Everything but Business Name and Phone can be left blank. No header row falls back to the fixed order Business Name, Contact Name, Phone, Industry, Email.

Or upload a CSV file. This opens a setup wizard where you match each column in your file to the right field yourself, so it can't get mismapped no matter how your export is laid out. Download a template.
